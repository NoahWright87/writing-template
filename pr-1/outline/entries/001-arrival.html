Chapter 1: Arrival

Logline

The crew of the Meridian picks up an impossible signal and Nova must decide, alone, whether to answer.

Treatment

Fourteen months into a routine deep-space survey, the Meridian's comms analyst flags an anomaly. What begins as a data artifact becomes, over the course of a single ship-day, the most consequential discovery in human history. The chapter ends before the response is sent — it ends at the moment Nova makes the decision.

Outline

  1. Cold open: the Meridian, fourteen months out, running on routine.
  2. The signal arrives during a shift-change scan. The analyst almost logs it as noise.
  3. Nova is woken. She reads the data without speaking.
  4. The crew convenes. Two factions form: respond immediately, or wait for peer review that will take years.
  5. Nova asks each person what they would do if the decision were theirs alone.
  6. She dismisses the crew. Alone in comms, she makes the call.
  7. Close on the transmission log: one line, sent. No take-backs.
