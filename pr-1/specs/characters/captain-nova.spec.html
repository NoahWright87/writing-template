Captain Nova

Summary

Commander of the deep-space survey vessel Meridian. Decisive under pressure, privately uncertain about almost everything else.

Description

Nova carries the weight of a mission that was supposed to be routine. She is not a hero in the classical sense — she makes the first-contact decision not because she is certain it is right, but because the alternative (not deciding) is its own irreversible act. Her arc is about the difference between authority and wisdom, and whether those can ever fully coincide.

Story Beats

  1. Introduced during a routine scan report — competent, slightly bored.
  2. First to suspect the signal is not natural; suppresses the thought until the data forces her hand.
  3. Mediates the crew argument; does not reveal her own position.
  4. Makes the decision alone, in the dark, without telling anyone until after.
  5. Spends the rest of the book waiting — and questioning whether waiting counts as living.
