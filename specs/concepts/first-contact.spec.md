# First Contact

## Summary
The moment a lone deep-space crew detects an unmistakably artificial signal and must decide, irreversibly, whether to respond.

## Description
First contact is the narrative hinge of the story. It transforms the crew from observers to participants. The decision to respond cannot be undone — the signal travels at light speed — making it the highest-stakes moment in the book. All character and thematic work before this point exists to prepare the reader to feel the weight of that choice.

## Story Beats

1. The signal arrives without warning during a routine scan.
2. The crew argues over whether it is artificial or a natural phenomenon.
3. Analysis confirms structure — it is unmistakably designed.
4. The captain must decide alone whether to respond before the window closes.
5. The response is sent. The crew waits. The waiting is the rest of the book.
